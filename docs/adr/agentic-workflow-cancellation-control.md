# Workflow cancellation/failure control (P6 A2b)

Status: internal opt-in, no production workflow runner wiring. This slice grants cancellation/failure accounting publication only. It does not grant provider, project-read, tool, model-operation or final-result authority. Existing synchronous nondurable coordinator and A2a raw storage APIs keep their behavior.

`createWorkflowCancellationControl` invokes the same genuine A2a initialization used by `createWorkflowLedgerJournal`, creates a new original durable ledger and exclusively owned W, and retains the raw session, ledger, journal, attempt, cursor and local queue privately. Its only returned value is an empty frozen object registered in a private WeakMap. A coordinator can claim that exact token once for its canonical original spec. Copies, raw A2a sessions and other specs cannot supply this authority. An unclaimed token can be closed without claiming a run.

The controlled coordinator separates business admission, accepted settlement, W attempt lifetime and storage resolution. Stage operations and event observers are registered with private accepted-work issuers before invocation. Their exact returned promises settle the leases. Cancellation, a deadline or stage failure synchronously seals admission, revokes the old W attempt and aborts the business signal. It does not block the original ledger, emit logical terminal status or release the project slot at that point. Direct `succeed` and `context.commit` throw before invoking effects because there is no completed-result authority in this slice.

Resolution is valid only after admission closure: an external call while admission is open rejects `accepted-work-not-sealed`. Resolution runs outside these leases. The registry rejects a stage or observer trying to await its own drain; the stage finally releases its actual lease before triggering outside resolution. Once business work and the local storage queue settle, resolution performs a fresh production full read fenced by the real writer owner before and after. `readOwned` checks owner exclusion, not attempt currentness, and is explicitly neither a queue barrier nor drain proof. This permits readback after a terminal manifest has made its producing attempt non-current.

The private storage controller compares the latest complete A1 prefix and original accounting with its live original ledger and accepted cursor. It issues a metadata-only attempt under the identical binding, appends recovery before any other new-epoch event, seals `cancelled` or `failed`, and publishes exactly that ledger component. Every append compares A2a's whole W outer-record bytes/hash/length/epoch/sequence with the genuine acknowledgement before accepting its A1 step. A rejected acknowledgement can be reconciled only by the exact genuine full persisted record; a replacement business ledger or all-state repair event is never created.

Only a matching real terminal manifest anchored at the latest complete journal, matching original spec/binding, exact state and sole ledger component is accepted. A flushed matching terminal wins over a lost publication acknowledgement or competing later reason. A seal without a manifest stays pending; a subsequent metadata-only retry preserves its fixed outcome and frozen accounting/time. Torn newest evidence, unavailable ownership or unreconciled local accounting stays pending with its project slot retained. Resolution never falls back to an older manifest, publishes `completed`, dispatches business work or drops holds.

After accepted storage resolution, the coordinator latches and emits the terminal once and waits terminal observer promises before releasing its exact project token. Old continuations cannot release a newer slot. `close()` waits the accepted leases and genuine W close/listener completion; closing pending storage does not turn it into an accepted terminal or release the project slot. The owned factory has no shared-owner or other-project entrance and never closes a supplied external owner.

Validation uses synthetic specs and actual Windows W/coordinator paths with a global zero-provider trap. Focused cases cover token identity, pending stages/observers, deadline/failure closure, acknowledgement loss, torn newest prefixes, fixed seals, self-wait, stale continuations, owner-only read semantics and actual listener closure. Existing A2a and nondurable coordinator tests remain required. This ADR does not claim M10/M11 completion; paid-operation lineage, final result publication, restart migration and production runner integration require separate contracts.
